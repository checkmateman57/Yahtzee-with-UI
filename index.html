Welcome to Yahtzee!

How To Play:
-Hit the roll button to change the dice values
-If you like your roll, click on dice to lock their value
-You can roll a max of 3 times before choosing how to score, which you can do by clicking the section

Rules:
-You get 3 rolls per turn, and can lock the die's value by clicking on the die.
-You must choose a section to get scored in each turn, even if it results in a 0

Scoring:
-There are 2 sections: an upper section and a lower section.
-The upper section consists of 1s, 2s, 3s, 4s, 5s, 6s, and is how many dice of that value you have added together.
(Ex. If you have 3 6s, thats 18 points.)
*If your score in the upper section exceeds 63, you get a 35 point bonus*

-The lower section consists of dice combinations
-3 of a kind and 4 of a kind means you must have 3 or 4 of the same value. All dice of that value are added together.
-Short straight is 4 in a row (30 points), Long straight is 5 in a row (40 points)
-Full House is having 3 dice of the same value, and another 2 dice with a different shared value (25 points)
-Chance is adding up all your dice into one score
-Yahtzee is when all 5 of your dice are the same value (50 points)

-The goal is to get the highest amount of points. Have Fun!
